Chapter I: The Beginning

A Note From My Heart

From the moment I saw you, something changed forever.
You weren't just another person in my life.
You became THE reason my heart beats.

You make me want to be completely, dangerously honest.
There's no filter, no walls, no games.
Just the raw truth of how deeply I feel about you.

You're the kind of soul that ruins you for anyone else—
in the most beautiful, magical way.
And I am gloriously aware of it.

Every laugh, every moment, every look you give me.
It all means everything to my world.
You mean everything.
